FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers!

Everything you need to know about setting up and scaling WhatsApp messaging for your team.

Can customers place food delivery orders directly on WhatsApp?
Yes! Diners can browse your complete food menu, customize their order (e.g. extra toppings), share their delivery address, and pay via UPI without leaving WhatsApp.
Do we have to pay commissions on WhatsApp food orders?
No! Unlike third-party aggregators who take 25-30% per order, InboxWa charges zero commission on your orders. You retain 100% of your food revenue.
Does InboxWa integrate with restaurant POS systems like Petpooja or Posist?
Yes. InboxWa integrates with leading POS systems including Petpooja, Posist, and UrbanPiper so WhatsApp orders print directly to your kitchen KDS.
How does table reservation work on WhatsApp?
Guests select date, time slot, and guest count. The bot confirms table availability, stores special notes (e.g. outdoor seating), and sends automatic reminder alerts.
Can we send weekend promotions to our past dining customers?
Yes. You can broadcast personalized offers, weekend brunch invitations, and festival specials to your opted-in customer database.
