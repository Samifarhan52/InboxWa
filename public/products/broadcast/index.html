What is WhatsApp Broadcasting?

WhatsApp broadcasting is a key feature of Meta's official business API through which you can send bulk messages at once. Each recipient receives messages in their own chat, without seeing others. It is an easy and effective way to share updates, offers, or alerts.

How to Broadcast Messages with HelloBots

#1

Create Your Target Audience

Begin by uploading a list of opted-in contacts in CSV form to HelloBots. Categorize your audience using tags and attributes, enabling you to differentiate customers based on their interests, location, or behavior. This helps your broadcasts reach the right audiences at the right moment for maximum results.

#2

Create or Use Pre-Approved Template

Choose a pre-approved WhatsApp message template from your library or create a new one for approval. Add personal touches to your messages by adding dynamic variables like the customer's name, location, or custom fields to make the broadcast more engaging and relevant to every recipient.

#3

Schedule or Publish Your Broadcast

You can send your broadcast right away or schedule it for later to align with customer timing. Scheduling gives you control of delivery time while ensuring each message reaches your audience at the right time and improves engagement.

#4

Monitor Performance

Once you send messages, it’s time to track the performance in real-time. With HelloBots, you can access detailed analytics about delivery status, click-through rate, and read receipts to measure the success and optimize the campaign for better results.
